## Part I — Program detail

### What Apex is

Apex · The Experiential Entrepreneurship Program is a **hybrid, cohort-based**
course that develops the *power skills* of the modern founder — emotionally
intelligent leadership, strategic communication, and digital scaffolding — through
one Orientation and five immersive phases. It is delivered on a **Reef-Native**
browser platform engineered for low-bandwidth, offline-tolerant use, so a learner on
a $200 Chromebook gets the same experience as one on a workstation.

The pedagogy rests on three commitments:

1. **Expose misconceptions before teaching skills.** The program opens by surfacing
   the learner's untested beliefs (Orientation), because skill instruction lands
   poorly on top of a confidently held false assumption.
2. **Learn by doing, then reflecting.** Each phase culminates in a live AI role-play
   followed by a Kolb-cycle debrief — experience *processed through reflection*, not
   experience alone (see `docs/experiential-learning-design.md`).
3. **Constructive alignment.** Every learning objective is both taught and assessed;
   nothing is graded that was not practiced, and nothing is practiced that does not
   ladder to an objective.

### Who it's for

Aspiring and early-stage founders, intrapreneurs, and career-changers who learn best
by doing. No prior business coursework is assumed; the Orientation levels the field.
Comfortable working in a browser; willing to be coached and to fail safely in
simulation.

### Program-level outcomes

By the end, a graduate can:

- Diagnose and regulate their own motivational and cognitive defaults under pressure.
- Build an evidence-based, iteratively-planned venture concept.
- Run an integrative negotiation that creates value and preserves the relationship.
- Lead a cross-functional team through uncertainty while holding psychological safety.
- Deliver an investor-grade pitch and negotiate a total-value compensation package.
- Operate a venture on Reef-Native principles: low-friction, performance-budgeted,
  offline-resilient.

### Format & cadence

- **Duration:** ~21 weeks. **~72 instructional hours.**
- **Structure:** 10 facilitated sessions (≈2–2.5 hrs each), roughly biweekly, with
  async platform work, simulations, and assessments between sessions.
- **Hybrid model:** every session runs **dual-mode** — in-room and remote learners
  participate equally (see the facilitator guide for the room/tech setup).
- **Class size:** 8–24 learners; simulations are 1:1 with the AI, so the model scales
  without adding facilitator load.

### Assessment philosophy

Formative assessments (including simulation debrief scores) are **low-stakes and
repeatable** — learners may "run it back." Each module has one **gating** assessment
(summative for Apex/Coral/Currents/School/Migration; the A1 conversation for
Orientation). The final pitch (`the-migration-A3`) is the **capstone**. Mastery, not
seat-time, is the unit of progress: a module unlocks the next when its completion
criteria are met (see `docs/program-manifest.md §3`).

### The metaphor system

Content is carried by the **Predator's Lexicon** — each phase has a sea-creature
totem encoding its lesson (the Lobster's molt = growth through discomfort; the Devil
Ray = riding the current in negotiation; the School = coordinated leadership). The
metaphor is a memory scaffold, not decoration: facilitators should invoke it when
naming principles in debriefs.

facilitator guide. "Async before" is platform/reading work due *before* the session;

### Session 1 — Shed the Shell *(Entering the Reef · LO1–LO6)*
- **Big question:** What do you believe you can't do — and is that a fact or a prediction?
- **Async before:** Create account; tour the platform; read the Orientation phase; skim the *Self-Limiting Belief Audit*.
- **In session:** Welcome & the molt metaphor → run the **Self-Limiting Belief Audit** live → the **Experiment Protocol** (make failure safe) → introduce the personal quality rubric → "chosen vs. inherited goals" pair exercise.
- **Do (simulation):** *The First Attempt* with coach **Maren Cole** (`entering-the-reef-S1`). 1:1, ~20 min.
- **Debrief & commit:** Each learner names one untested belief, one 24-hour experiment, and one "still-holding" question.
- **Async after (A1, A2):** Complete the **A1** coaching conversation to score ≥6; submit the **A2** experiment log (attempt + expected-vs-actual + ability/persistence/approach diagnosis + self-rubric).

### Session 2 — Reading the Water *(Apex Positioning · LO1, LO2)*
- **Big question:** When the stakes rise, do you chase the win or flee the loss — and is this game zero-sum or shared?
- **Async before:** Apex Positioning phase; take the regulatory-focus self-diagnostic (A1).
- **In session:** Promotion vs. prevention focus and its failure modes → the *Winning vs. Not Losing* checklist → zero-sum vs. collaborative classification drill (A2) with the Apex Mentor probing strategy choices.
- **Do:** Mini-cases — classify three deals by structure; name the safeguard each focus needs.
- **Commit:** Each learner states their default focus and the safeguard they'll install.
- **Async after (A1, A2):** Finalize the focus diagnostic + written rationale; complete the scenario-classification set.

### Session 3 — Position vs. Interest *(Apex Positioning · LO3 → gating A3)*
- **Big question:** What is the other side *actually* protecting beneath the number they said?
- **Async before:** Positions-vs-interests reading; the $12,000-vs-quick-close worksheet.
- **In session:** Separating positions from interests → evidence-and-confirmation move → set up the simulation.
- **Do (simulation):** *The Dock Deal* with **Dale Mercer** (`apex-positioning-S1`), then the four-dimension debrief and score; reach 6/8 to unlock.
- **Debrief & commit:** Name the interest beneath Dale's $8,400 and the exact words to use before talking numbers next time.
- **Async after (A3, summative):** Short written analysis separating positions from interests in a provided transcript; states winning vs. not-losing. **Gates Module 1.**

### Session 4 — The Evolving Document *(Coral Scaffolding · LO1, LO2)*
- **Big question:** How do you get from a rough idea to a fundable plan without freezing on the blank page?
- **Async before:** Coral Scaffolding phase; capture a venture idea by **voice-to-text** (BizChat low-floor entry).
- **In session:** BizChat methodology (low-floor / high-ceiling / just-in-time feedback) → draft the **executive summary** and begin **market analysis** live, using AI deepening/bridging prompts.
- **Do:** Iterate one section twice; observe expected-vs-actual quality lift (ties back to Orientation's "push past the first draft").
- **Commit:** A one-paragraph value proposition + the single riskiest assumption to research.
- **Async after (A1, A3):** Continue building the iterative plan; draft three expert-directed help-seeking questions.

### Session 5 — Evidence & the Ask *(Coral Scaffolding · LO2, LO3 → gating A2)*
- **Big question:** Does your market analysis rest on evidence — and do you know how to ask an expert for what you actually need?
- **Async before:** Complete a draft business plan (exec summary, market analysis, future-of-the-company).
- **In session:** Evidence standards for market claims → **help-seeking skills** workshop (sharpen "questions to ask an expert"; reduce reputational risk) → structured peer critique of plans.
- **Do:** Live "ask an expert" round-robin using each learner's sharpest question.
- **Async after (A2, summative):** Submit the iterative business-plan artifact. **Gates Module 2.**

### Session 6 — Expand the Pie *(Navigating the Currents · LO1, LO2)*
- **Big question:** How do you create value in a deal instead of just splitting it?
- **Async before:** Navigating the Currents phase; identify your BATNA for a given scenario.
- **In session:** Rapport-first research finding → BATNA analysis → active-listening paraphrase → **MESOs** (Multiple Equivalent Simultaneous Offers) to reveal preferences.
- **Do:** Paired MESO drill; partners infer each other's hidden preferences from offer choice.
- **Commit:** Draft three equivalent offers for an upcoming real negotiation.
- **Async after (A1):** Formative rapport/BATNA exercise on the platform.

### Session 7 — Hold the Anchor *(Navigating the Currents · LO3 → gating A2)*
- **Big question:** When they open with an aggressive number, how do you neutralize it without conceding?
- **Async before:** Counter-anchoring reading ("explain how you arrived at that value").
- **In session:** Anchoring effect → the counter-anchoring move → set up the integrative-negotiation simulation.
- **Do (simulation):** `navigating-the-currents-S1` role-play + Kolb debrief and score.
- **Debrief & commit:** Name where the pie expanded and the one move that created the most value.
- **Async after (A2, summative):** Integrative-negotiation performance assessment. **Gates Module 3.**

### Session 8 — Safe to Speak *(Schooling Strategy · LO1, LO2)*
- **Big question:** What makes a team able to disagree productively and recover when communication breaks?
- **Async before:** Schooling Strategy phase; read the *Conversational Repair* strategy guide.
- **In session:** **Psychological safety**, **purposeful inquiry**, **productive disagreement** → conversational-repair techniques (keyword highlighting, options, explanations, escalation) → role-played breakdown-and-repair rounds.
- **Do:** Triad exercise — one breakdown scenario, each learner deploys a different repair strategy; compare outcomes.
- **Commit:** One phrase each learner will use to invite dissent on their team.
- **Async after (A1, A2):** Formative psychological-safety and repair exercises.

### Session 9 — Move as One → Total Value *(Schooling Strategy LO3 + The Migration LO1, LO2)*
- **Big question:** How do you align several functions under pressure — and how do you value an offer beyond its base pay?
- **Async before:** Finish Schooling Strategy; begin The Migration phase (compensation + Reef-Native ops).
- **In session (Part A):** Design a **cross-functional coordination plan** under a time/resource constraint (School A3 setup). **(Part B):** Total-compensation thinking with 2026 salary-guide data; the **Reef-Native operational philosophy** (browser-native, performance budgets, offline-first).
- **Do:** Build a one-page coordination plan; draft a justified compensation counter.
- **Async after (School A3 summative; Migration A1, A2):** Submit the coordination plan + leadership rationale (**gates Module 4**); complete the compensation-evaluation and Reef-Native-spec exercises.

### Session 10 — Demo Day *(The Migration · LO3 → capstone A3)*
- **Big question:** Can you make an investor remember you in three minutes?
- **Async before:** Build the pitch; rehearse against **AI feedback** (listenability & volume, hesitation words, pace, eye contact).
- **In session:** Final rehearsal & feedback → **Demo Day** live pitches to a panel (in-room + remote judges) → compensation-negotiation lightning rounds → program close and the "open ocean" send-off.
- **Do (capstone):** Deliver the final pitch; **A3 capstone** assessed.
- **Commit:** A 90-day post-program action plan; one experiment to run in week one (closing the loop opened in Session 1).
- **Async after:** Capstone scoring, graduation, and reading-list handoff (`docs/further-reading.md`).
